I'm Omar, a PhD student in Computer Science (approximation algorithms) who got into offensive security. I have had a general idea
about cybersecurity over my studies (general Computer Security course, and a Binary Exploitation lab that I barely passed), but I
started taking it seriously in early 2025. 

Around mid-2026, I decided to take the CPTS exam by Hack The Box, and passed the exam in October 2026 in my first attempt, retrieving all 14 flags, exceeding the required 12. In the process of preparing and doing the exam, I had to learn to deal with burnout, frustration, time- and energy-management as much as I learned about Active Directory, web exploitation and privilege escalation.

In this blog, I plan to write about cybersecurity, including both technical and non-technical aspects:
- Write-ups for (retired) Hack The Box machines and challenges, focusing on methodology, what I tried, and where to look for clues rather than just straightforward paths
- Notes on techniques ranging from beginner level to advanced topics as I learn them
- Personal notes on learning cybersecurity and doing side-projects alongside a full-time PhD position, avoiding burnout and being more balanced.

I am by no means an expert about the topics I discuss in this blog; I am learning as I go. I own my mistakes, and I'd appreciate your (constructive) feedback.
